Mathematics · Princeton University

Zachary Lihn

I am a PhD student in mathematics at Princeton University, advised by Camillo De Lellis at the Institute for Advanced Study.

My research interests lie in geometric analysis, particularly the regularity of area-minimizing currents, minimal surfaces, and geometric partial differential equations.

I received my BA in mathematics with honors from Columbia University in 2025, where I wrote my senior thesis under the supervision of Duong Phong. I am supported by an NSF Graduate Research Fellowship.
