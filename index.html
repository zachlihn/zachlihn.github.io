Zachary Lihn

Hi! I am a 2nd year PhD student in mathematics at Princeton University, advised by Camillo De Lellis at the Institute for Advanced Study.

My research interests lie in geometric analysis, particularly the regularity of area-minimizing currents, minimal surfaces, and geometric partial differential equations.

I received my BA in mathematics from Columbia University in 2025, where I wrote my senior thesis in complex geometry under the supervision of Duong Phong. I am supported by an NSF Graduate Research Fellowship.
